# Resource Ledger Expansion — Entity Research and Account-Owner Data

## Scope

This expansion adds **ten direct, reviewed resources** to the Ontario Research Hub’s source ledger. It supports lawful business or entity research and an individual’s review of their **own** information. It does not add people-search, social-profile discovery, contact enrichment, monitoring, device, credential, financial, tracking, scraping, or evasion tools.

## Reviewed additions

| Resource | Directory category | Safe use note | Verification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Canada’s Business Registries | Public records | Use the official registry named in a result for material verification; the service forbids automated copying or scraping. | [1] |
| Corporations Canada — Federal Corporation Search | Public records | Use for federal corporations only; provincial, financial, and foreign corporations are out of scope. | [2] |
| CRA List of Charities | Public records | Use for organization-level charity status research and review the CRA’s current information. | [3] |
| Canadian Trademarks Database | Regulatory | Use as an intellectual-property reference and verify current status before acting. | [4] |
| Canadian Patents Database | Regulatory | Use to locate published Canadian patent records; record the publication number and status date. | [5] |
| Ontario IPC — Access and Correction Rights | Self-service privacy | Use to request or correct **your own** information or narrowly scoped public-sector records. | [6] |
| Privacy Commissioner of Canada — Access Your Information | Self-service privacy | Use for a request for **your own** personal information held by eligible private-sector organizations. | [7] |
| Google Takeout — Your Account Data | Self-service privacy | Use only to export and review data associated with your own Google account. | [8] |
| LinkedIn — Download Your Account Data | Self-service privacy | Use only to obtain your own account archive through platform controls. | [9] |
| Facebook — Export Your Information | Self-service privacy | Use only to export your own account information and handle any archive responsibly. | [10] |

## Curation decision

The resources are direct issuing-institution or platform-owner links. Canada’s Business Registries explicitly directs users to the official registry for complete and current business information and prohibits automated copying or scraping. [1] Corporations Canada similarly states that its search is limited to corporations created under federal corporate law. [2] Ontario IPC and the federal Privacy Commissioner both frame access around a person’s own personal information, which is why the related entries are labeled **Self-service privacy** rather than OSINT or person research. [6] [7]
