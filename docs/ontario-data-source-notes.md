# Ontario Data Source Notes

## Curated Official Sources

| Source | Purpose for the Ontario Data page | Scope and use note |
|---|---|---|
| [Ontario Data Catalogue](https://data.ontario.ca/) | Province-wide open-data discovery. | The catalogue lists Government of Ontario datasets across major policy domains. Review the specific dataset licence, coverage, and methodology before reuse. |
| [Ontario GeoHub](https://geohub.lio.gov.on.ca/) | Authoritative provincial geospatial data and mapping applications. | GeoHub offers geography, imagery, elevation, water, forestry, and other spatial resources. Its terms caution that content can be incomplete or change over time. |
| [2021 Census Profile — Ontario](https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/2021/dp-pd/prof/search-recherche/lst/results-resultats.cfm?Lang=E&GEOCODE=35) | Official aggregate population and geography information. | The profile supports province, community, and census-geography browsing; the page highlights that its results are presented at geographic levels. |
| [Ontario climate projections dataset](https://data.ontario.ca/dataset/climate-data-high-resolution-projections) | Entry point for provincial climate-projection information. | The page is described as high-resolution projection data; modeled scenarios should not be presented as observed conditions. |
| [Ontario Health open data](https://www.ontariohealth.ca/system/data/open.html) | Ontario Health’s data-release inventory. | Use published aggregate information only and do not attempt to identify individuals from health data. |

## Municipal Coverage

The dedicated page reuses the existing city-filtered municipal open-data links already maintained in the Source Ledger for Toronto, Ottawa, Hamilton, Mississauga, and Brampton. These links remain direct outbound routes to the issuer’s portal rather than a proxy or data-copying service.
