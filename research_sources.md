# Verified Ontario legal and public-records sources

| Source | Official destination | Scope and access note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario Land Registry / OnLand | <https://www.onland.ca/ui/> | ServiceOntario states that the registry holds official property-ownership and legal-interest records, including mortgages, transfers, and leases. OnLand can search, view, and download current and historical property records; self-searches carry a fee. |
| Personal Property Security Registration / Access Now | <https://www.ontario.ca/page/register-security-interest-or-search-lien-access-now> | Access Now provides online access to Ontario’s personal-property security registration system for lien and security-interest matters. The official page distinguishes it from real-property and land searches; users may need an account or deposit account for some actions. |
| Law Society of Ontario Lawyer and Paralegal Directory | <https://lsodirectory.lso.ca/en-US/> | The Law Society’s official directory can be used to check whether a person is or was licensed, find business contact information, and see practising status. It is a regulatory directory and does not provide legal advice. |
| Ontario business licence, registration, or appointment directory | <https://www.ontario.ca/page/search-business-licence-registration-or-appointment> | The provincial directory covers specified regulated business categories, including bailiff businesses, collection agencies, consumer reporting agencies, and payday lenders/loan brokers. It also contains limited employee/appointment listings and notes that the list is updated monthly. |

The website will present these as direct external sources with a specific scope note and no claim of legal interpretation, ownership verification, or personal profiling.
