# Privacy & Personal Security Directory Review

## Curation boundary

The supplied Start.me directory is an untrusted third-party index. It includes defensive privacy and security references alongside anonymous-proxy, Tor, virtual-machine, temporary-email, leak-search, fake-identity, fake-content, account, and other material that does not fit the Ontario Research Hub’s privacy-first public-source model.

The Hub will retain only individually reviewed, **defensive** references that help users protect their own accounts, assess public claims, or understand authorized local security practices. It will not reproduce destinations for anonymity or identity evasion, temporary/disposable accounts, leak or credential searching, fake identity/content generation, person tracking, password recovery, account access, or other abuse-enabling activity.

## Initial eligible categories

| Directory category | Potential treatment |
|---|---|
| Secure browsers/search | Retain individually verified privacy-preserving browsers or search providers; exclude anonymizing proxies, Tor/evasion services, and browser-hosted virtual environments. |
| Disinformation and fact checking | Retain individually verified public verification and media-literacy references. |
| Password managers | Retain official password-manager education or reputable providers as defensive personal-security references. |
| Authorized local testing | Link back to the existing local-only Research Sandbox rather than adding external remote virtual-device or VM services. |

## Excluded categories observed

Anonymous proxies, Tor/evasion links, virtual browser sessions, open-cloud/virtual-machine services, temporary and anonymous email services, credential/leak checks, fake identity/voice/name/photo/account tools, fake content tools, dark-web/data-breach collections, and any tracking or account-access content are excluded.

## Verified additions

| Included destination | Curated category | Verified defensive purpose | In-app boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| [DuckDuckGo](https://duckduckgo.com/) | Private browsing | The publisher describes private search and browsing protections. | Protect your own browsing; do not use privacy features for evasion or targeting. |
| [Startpage](https://www.startpage.com/) | Private browsing | The publisher describes private, unprofiled search and a no-search-history approach. | Follow source terms and applicable law. |
| [Google Fact Check Explorer](https://toolbox.google.com/factcheck/explorer) | Information verification | A public fact-check search tool. | Review primary sources, dates, methodology, and context. |
| [Verification Handbook](https://verificationhandbook.com/) | Information verification | A public guide to verifying digital user-generated content. | Do not identify, track, or expose private individuals. |
| [KeePass](https://keepass.info/) | Password hygiene | An open-source password manager. | Use only for credentials you own or administer. |
| [1Password](https://1password.com/) | Password hygiene | A credential-management platform. | The Hub never requests or retains credentials. |
| [Sync](https://www.sync.com/) | Private storage & communication | A privacy-oriented encrypted storage and backup provider. | Confirm storage, sharing, retention, and legal requirements before use. |
| [Matrix](https://matrix.org/) | Private storage & communication | An open, decentralized communications network. | Do not use communications tools for harassment, impersonation, or evasion. |
| [Google Results About You](https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/12719076) | Digital privacy review | A self-directed path to review whether your own contact information appears in Search results and request eligible removals. | Do not use it for another person; a Search removal does not necessarily remove the source page. |
| [EFF Cover Your Tracks](https://coveryourtracks.eff.org/) | Digital privacy review | A self-directed browser-tracking and fingerprint-awareness test. | Run it only on your own browser and never use fingerprint data to profile others. |

The ten references above are the only individually verified links retained from the 238-link directory review. All other groups remain excluded or unlisted because they are outside the Hub’s defensive, privacy-first scope.
